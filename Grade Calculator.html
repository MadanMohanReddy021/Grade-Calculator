How the score is calculated

Theory = Sessional 1 × 30% + Sessional 2 × 45% + LE × 25%, rounded up to the next whole point.

With a lab: final score = theory × 70% + lab × 30%, rounded up. Scores map to grades O 10, A+ 9, A 8, B+ 7, B 6, C 5.
